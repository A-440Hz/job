# Job Application Tracker With Lootboxes and Agentic Functionality

- Track the jobs you apply to.

- Use AI to summarize job descriptions directly from their listing urls. 

- Set your own goal for earning lootboxes. 

- Save your progress across multiple devices.

- Maintain streaks. Open rare Squids.

- Take control of your career. Earn a stable salary. Seize your destiny.


## How to use:

Click the gear icon to open the tracker settings menu.

Here you can set the number of applications you would like to make to earn a lootbox.

Additionally set whether your cumulative progress resets daily or weekly.

Changes you make are highlighted in yellow. Click "Save Changes" to confirm them.

![tracker settings menu opened](/frontend/public/tt1.png)

Apply for jobs and track your applications by hitting the "new application" button.

Fill in at least the title element (*\*Company - Position*) before saving.

![new application starts a new application](/frontend/public/tt2.png)

Press the AI button to try scraping any job listing url and summarizing the position details...

![AI button with a valid url](/frontend/public/tt3.png)

Or leave url blank and press the AI button to directly summarize any job description pasted in the notes section.

![AI button with a blank url and valid notes](/frontend/public/tt4.png)

Select your preferred AI model for optimal results.

![select AI model from settings menu](/frontend/public/tt5.png)

Will you find a job before successfully earning all 100+ Squids? There's only one way to find out!

![earn all the squids!](/frontend/public/tt6.png)
